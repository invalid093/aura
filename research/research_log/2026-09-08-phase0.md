## RL-0001 — Replace the original research question

**Date:** 2026-09-08
**Decision:** Retire the original framing (*how can an autonomous aircraft detect, isolate and
respond to degradation when human intervention is unavailable?*) and adopt RQ-1.
**Rationale:** The original contained at least four separable research programmes, specified no
comparison, and had no falsification condition. It is an engineering prompt, not a hypothesis.
**Evidence:** `reports/phase0/RESEARCH_DIRECTION_COMPARISON.md`; six candidate directions scored.
**Impact:** Scope narrowed substantially. Fault-tolerant control, prognostics, planning and
human-subject work moved out of scope.
**Next step:** Verify novelty by systematic search before any claim (TV-N1).
**Record:** `docs/decisions/ADR-0002-research-question.md`

## RL-0002 — Adopt a published adversarial result as the falsification condition

**Date:** 2026-09-08
**Decision:** Take the estimator-equivalence finding of LIT-0012 (Gaus, Charaja & Haeufle, 2026) as
H1's falsification criterion rather than arguing around it.
**Rationale:** That study compared seven uncertainty estimators under threshold-gated deferral and
found act/defer agreement above 97.8%, with the threshold dominating the estimator and semantic OOD
detection near chance. It is a direct prediction that AURA's central idea will not work. Adopting it
as the criterion is what makes H1 a hypothesis rather than a plan.
**Evidence:** LIT-0012, full text retrieved and extracted.
**Impact:** H1 now has a concrete, externally-derived way to fail. The stated reason AURA might
differ — that its two components read structurally different evidence — is recorded explicitly as an
argument, not a result.
**Next step:** Design the decision-agreement metric so the equivalence test is symmetric with theirs.

## RL-0003 — Derive the primary secondary hypothesis from a competitor's stated limitation

**Date:** 2026-09-08
**Decision:** Adopt H3 (novelty gating suppresses genuine fault detection as shift grows) as the
hypothesis most likely to yield a clean result.
**Rationale:** LIT-0004 (Mohammadi, Krysander, Jung & Frisk, 2025) already performs uncertainty-aware,
epistemic-gated, consistency-based diagnosis with abstention — which closes most of the obvious
contribution. But it states, without quantifying, that OOD detection can suppress fault-induced
anomalies when an operating-point shift coincides with a fault. That quantity is well-defined,
well-motivated, unmeasured, and **method-independent**.
**Evidence:** LIT-0004, full text retrieved; stated limitation reproduced in
`reports/phase0/LITERATURE_REVIEW.md` §5.
**Impact:** The programme now yields a result whether or not AURA's own method succeeds. LIT-0004 is
simultaneously the main novelty threat and the decisive baseline (B4).
**Next step:** Implement B4 faithfully enough that beating it means something.

## RL-0004 — Use structural isolability as ground truth for uncertainty

**Date:** 2026-09-08
**Decision:** Score the evidential-ambiguity component against structural isolability computed per
flight condition.
**Rationale:** Uncertainty-quantification research is normally handicapped by having no reference
for what the "correct" uncertainty is. Structural analysis supplies a computable, mode-dependent,
model-independent lower bound on ambiguity.
**Evidence:** LIT-0001, LIT-0002 (toolbox, retrieved), LIT-0068 (mode-dependent test selection).
Caveat recorded as A-UNC-03: structural conditions are necessary, not sufficient — isolability
*bounds* ambiguity rather than determining it.
**Impact:** This is the most distinctive idea in the design, and it introduces a single point of
failure: it requires isolability to actually vary with flight condition.
**Next step:** **EXP-0002.** Nothing else proceeds until it passes.

## RL-0005 — Exclude GPS from the sensor suite, with a mandatory sensitivity run

**Date:** 2026-09-08
**Decision:** Adopt a 13-channel suite without GPS or magnetometer, and commit in advance to
repeating the primary comparison with GPS included.
**Rationale:** A fully redundant suite would make every fault isolable and leave H2 nothing to
measure. But excluding a sensor *because* including it weakens the effect under study is exactly
how a non-generalising result gets produced.
**Evidence:** A-SEN-01, recorded as the most questionable choice in the design.
**Impact:** If the advantage disappears with GPS included, the honest finding is that the
decomposition matters only in under-determined sensor suites — and that becomes the headline rather
than a footnote.
**Next step:** EXP-0009 sensitivity run; EXP-0002 first confirms the suite gives a non-degenerate
isolability structure.
**Record:** `docs/decisions/ADR-0005-sensor-suite.md`

## RL-0006 — No licence during Phase 0

**Date:** 2026-09-08
**Decision:** Remove the MIT licence added during initial setup; the repository carries no licence.
**Rationale:** It was chosen as a convention rather than decided. Outbound licensing cannot
responsibly be set before the inbound obligations of the aircraft model, structural-analysis toolbox
and candidate dataset are verified.
**Evidence:** Open actions A-1, A-2, A-6 — all three licences unverified.
**Impact:** No reuse permission is offered or implied. The question must be revisited before
publishing a benchmark artefact or making an artefact-availability statement.
**Next step:** Verify the three external licences; then decide code, data and documentation
licensing separately.
**Record:** `docs/decisions/ADR-0006-licensing-deferred.md`

## Methodological reservations carried into Phase 1

Recorded because they should constrain how results are read, not because they are resolved:

- Only 3 of 70 literature sources were read in full; the gap matrix rests largely on abstract-level
  evidence.
- Residual simulation bias cannot be eliminated — the strongest OOD axis still sits inside one

  falsified and the secondary one supported.
