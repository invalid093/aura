# FAIL-0001 — Flight condition FC-4 departs controlled flight; matrix invalid

- **What failed:** Flight condition FC-4 (5000 m, 24 m/s) in EXP-0002. The nominal,
  fault-free closed-loop run does not hold trim: it stalls and departs.
- **When:** 2026-09-08, EXP-0002 primary grid, commit `f030749`.
- **Category:** `EXPERIMENTAL_INVALIDITY`

## Evidence

Nominal (F0) run at FC-4, measured over the 20 s scenario:

| Quantity | FC-1 (valid) | FC-4 |
|---|---|---|
| trim α | −0.88° | 15.92° |
| **max α** | −0.04° | **31.52°** |
| max \|θ − θ_trim\| | 2.86° | **32.47°** |
| max \|V − V_trim\| | 0.86 m/s | **27.73 m/s** |
| max \|h − h_trim\| | 5.3 m | **353.3 m** |

The stall blend in the GFW-1 lift model is centred at 20°. FC-4 drives the aircraft
to 31.5° — well beyond it — while airspeed more than doubles and the aircraft loses
353 m. **This is a stall/dive event, not a trimmed manoeuvre with a small doublet.**

## Why it produced a plausible-looking result

FC-4 returned **153/153 pairs distinguishable** — a perfectly diagonal matrix — with
median distance 18.8 versus 3.2–3.4 at the valid conditions, and Spearman rank
correlation of only 0.37–0.46 against them.

Read naively this is the strongest possible evidence for the experiment's hypothesis:
maximal condition-dependence. It is not. **A divergent nonlinear trajectory amplifies
any perturbation**, so every fault separates from every other for reasons that have
nothing to do with diagnosability. The low rank correlation reflects FC-4 being a
*different phenomenon*, not a different operating point.

**This is the failure mode the validity attack exists to catch, and it would have
produced a confidently wrong headline result.**

## Scientific impact

- The FC-4 matrix and every quantity derived from it are **excluded** from the
  EXP-0002 determination.
- The cross-condition evidence must be re-derived from valid conditions only. This
  substantially **weakens** the apparent condition-dependence: among FC-1/2/3 the
  binary verdict flips on only 1 of 153 pairs, and Spearman correlations are
  0.85–0.98 rather than 0.37–0.46.
- No prior conclusion is affected, because none had been published: the failure was
  found during the validity attack, before interpretation.

## Root cause: a defect in the pilot design, not in the model

The pilot (`pilot_verify.py`) checked closed-loop boundedness **at FC-1 only**. Trim
converged at all four conditions with tiny residuals, and trim convergence was
mistaken for flyability. Trim is an equilibrium existence check; it says nothing about
whether the closed loop can hold that equilibrium through the commanded manoeuvre.

## Methodological implication

A **validity gate** is now part of the experiment pipeline rather than an
after-the-fact check. Every flight condition must pass, on its nominal run:

| Criterion | Threshold |
|---|---|
| no divergence | — |
| max α | < 12° (the linear region; blend centre is 20°) |
| max \|θ − θ_trim\| | < 10° |
| max \|V − V_trim\| | < 3 m/s |
| max \|h − h_trim\| | < 50 m |

These thresholds were declared **before** any replacement condition's matrix was
computed. They test whether the aircraft is flying a controlled trimmed manoeuvre —
not whether the result is interesting.

## Resolution

1. FC-4 is retained in the record, marked `INVALID`, and reported. It is not deleted.
2. An envelope scan over airspeed at both altitudes identified the highest-α condition
   that remains controllable. **FC-5 (5000 m, 28 m/s; trim α = 10.15°, max α = 11.65°,
   q̄ = 289 Pa)** replaces FC-4 as the high-α probe.
3. The replacement was selected by the validity rule above, applied before its
   distinguishability matrix was computed. It is a repair of an experimental defect,
   **not** a search for a favourable condition — a distinction a reviewer should check
   by re-running the scan.
4. The validity gate now runs inside `run_exp0002.py` and marks any failing condition
   `INVALID` automatically.

## Became an engineering test (2026-09-08)

This research failure is now enforced automatically by the AURA framework rather than by
researcher vigilance:

- **Gate:** `model_validity_envelope` (RUNTIME) rejects any run whose observations leave
  the envelope declared in its specification, marking it `INVALID` and emitting **no**
  scientific conclusion.
- **Specification:** `model.validity_envelope` is a required field for exploratory and
  confirmatory experiments; `aura.validation` refuses a specification without one.
- **Regression tests:**
  - `tests/test_core.py::TestGates::test_envelope_violation_is_invalid`
  - `tests/test_core.py::TestGates::test_envelope_missing_observation_raises` — an
    unobserved envelope variable cannot be certified, so the gate raises rather than
    passing.
  - `tests/test_pipeline.py::TestRunLifecycle::test_envelope_violation_invalidates_and_records_failure`
- **Live demonstration:** `DEMO-0002` reproduces the failure mode deliberately and is
  rejected automatically (`FAIL-0002`).

The specific hazard FAIL-0001 exposed — that the invalid condition produced the *most
favourable-looking* numbers in the study — is why the gate is unconditional and why a
rejected run's report contains an explicitly empty "what the evidence supports" section
rather than omitting it.
